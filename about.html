FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Ask a safari expert.

What's included in the price?

Your guide and 4x4 vehicle, accommodation, park and conservancy fees, most meals, airport transfers and drinking water on game drives. International flights, visas, travel insurance, tips and optional activities (like balloon safaris) are extra. Each trip lists exactly what's included.

Do I need a visa?

Most visitors need an eTA for Kenya and an e-Visa for Tanzania and Uganda; Rwanda offers visa on arrival. The East Africa Tourist Visa covers Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda on one visa. Rules change, so always check the official government sites before you travel. We'll send links when you book.

Which vaccinations do I need?

Yellow fever vaccination is recommended, and a certificate can be required, for example when travelling between Kenya and Tanzania or Zanzibar. Malaria prevention is advised in most safari areas. Please see a travel clinic 6–8 weeks before departure.

How much does a gorilla permit cost?

Permit prices are set by each government and are included in our gorilla trips. At the time of writing they're around $800 per person in Uganda and $1,500 in Rwanda. We confirm the current price when we quote.

Is travel insurance required?

Yes. Comprehensive travel insurance covering medical evacuation is required for all our trips. Every trip also includes emergency flying-doctor cover while you're on safari.

How big are the groups?

Small-group trips have a maximum of 12 travellers (8 on gorilla trips), and everyone gets a guaranteed window seat. Private trips are just you and your party.

What are your booking conditions?

A $250 deposit secures most trips, with the balance due 60 days before departure. Free date changes up to 60 days out. Full terms are sent with every quote.
