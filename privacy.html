Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information SafariNomad ("we") collects through this website, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have. We follow Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we collect

  • Details you send us in a booking request, quote request, custom trip, group request, flight request or newsletter sign-up: for example your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, country, passport nationality, travel dates, number of travellers, and whether you need help with visas or flights.
  • Progress on longer forms. On the booking, customise-your-trip and group pages, once you have typed an email address or phone number we save what you have entered so far, so a planner can help if you get stuck or leave before sending. The page tells you this beside the form.
  • Questions you ask Nomad, our AI travel assistant, and its answers, so we can improve the answers and help you further.
  • Anonymous visit statistics: the pages viewed, packages opened, words typed in the site search, the website you came from, and the time zone and language your browser reports. These are counted as daily totals only. We do not store your IP address and we do not use tracking cookies.

What stays on your device

Your chosen language and currency, saved trips, recently viewed trips and unsent form drafts are kept in your own browser's storage so the site remembers them. A small flag in the current browser tab is used to count a visit once. You can clear all of this in your browser settings.

Why we use your information

  • To answer your request, prepare quotes and itineraries, and arrange and manage your booking.
  • To follow up if you started a booking or request and did not finish it.
  • To send the newsletter or flash-deal alerts you asked for (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To understand how the site is used and make it better.

Who we share it with

  • Suppliers needed for your trip, such as lodges, camps, hotels, airlines, park authorities and permit offices, and only what they need.
  • Our AI provider. Nomad's answers are generated by Google Gemini, so the questions you type are sent to Google to produce a reply and may be used by Google under its own terms. Please don't type passport numbers or payment details into the chat.
  • Flight data. When you check a flight's status, the flight number, airport and date are sent to our licensed flight-data provider. No personal details are sent.
  • We never sell your personal information.

Payments

This website does not take card payments. Deposits and balances are arranged directly with our team after your booking is confirmed.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as needed to deliver your trip and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. Unfinished requests and chat questions are kept only as long as they are useful for following up and improving our service, and are deleted on request.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how we use it. Email and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
