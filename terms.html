Booking Terms

Last updated: 11 November 2026

These terms explain how booking a trip with SafariNomad works. Your written booking confirmation sets out the final details of your trip, and it takes priority if anything differs.

1. Booking requests

When you send a booking request on this website, no payment is taken. A planner checks availability and confirms your booking within 24 hours. Your booking is only confirmed once you receive our written confirmation.

2. Prices

Prices are per person in US dollars (the site can also show euros as a guide). The price in your confirmation is final. Prices for private trips, custom trips and groups are confirmed in your quote. You can ask for a discount when you book; a discount applies only once we confirm it in writing.

3. Deposit and balance

  • A deposit of per person secures most trips (never more than the trip's price).
  • The balance is due days before departure.
  • Gorilla permits are set by each government and must be paid in full when you book, because permits are issued in advance.
  • Trips can start no sooner than days after booking.

4. Changing your dates

Date changes are free up to days before you travel, subject to availability. Later changes depend on our suppliers and are confirmed case by case.

5. Cancellations

If you need to cancel, tell us in writing as soon as possible. The cancellation charges that apply to your trip are set out in your booking confirmation.

6. What's included

Unless your confirmation says otherwise, safaris include your guide and 4x4 vehicle, accommodation, park and conservancy fees, most meals, airport transfers and drinking water on game drives. International flights, visas, travel insurance, tips and optional extras (such as balloon safaris) are not included.

7. Travel insurance and health

Comprehensive travel insurance with medical evacuation cover is required for every traveller. Every safari also includes flying-doctor emergency cover while you are on safari. You are responsible for any vaccinations or health documents your destinations require.

8. Passports and visas

You are responsible for valid passports and visas or eTAs for every country on your trip. We can guide you through the process, but entry decisions are made by each country's authorities.

9. Children

Children aged 5 to 15 can travel from % off on selected trips. Gorilla trekking has a minimum age of 15.
